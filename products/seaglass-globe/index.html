The spec sheet

What sits under the picture.

> spec
Datum
WGS-84 ellipsoid · geodetic ↔ ECEF in f64, precision held relative to the camera
Geodesy
Karney geodesic and area (GeographicLib) · Vincenty under symbol anchors, with a Karney fallback for near-antipodal pairs
View
globe and flat projections, switched live
GPU
Rust · wgpu · Vulkan or DX12; needs a real GPU, which a remote-desktop session usually can't drive
Data
XYZ · WMS · WMTS · MVT · 3D Tiles · glTF · GeoJSON · KML · Shapefile

The engines are headless, and ForgeGIS Studio™ is where a person operates them on a flat map. When the Globe is running it is the agent’s presentation surface: visual answers — contours, elevation profiles, draped rasters — are drawn on the globe instead of only described, and the suite’s GPU engine, ForgeGIS™, runs from a workflow editor docked under the map.

A note on licensing. Seaglass Globe is an opt-in component of the Forge suite, installed by the Seaglass Control Center™, and is not sold separately.
