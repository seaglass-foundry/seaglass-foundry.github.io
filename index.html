ForgeGIS

GPU geospatial compute for Java.

ForgeGIS™ runs terrain, hydrology, visibility, spectral and vector operations on the GPU, from Java or from an AI agent over MCP. Checked against GDAL, and faster.

Seaglass Globe

From orbit to the rooftops.

Seaglass Globe™ is a WGS-84 virtual globe in double precision, with terrain, 3D buildings, geodesy that names its method and MIL-STD-2525C scenarios on a timeline. The suite’s optional 3D view.
